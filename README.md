# My Favorite Movie: Toy Story 

## About the Movie

**Toy Story** is an animated movie about toys that come to life whenever humans are not around. It follows the adventures of *Woody*, *Buzz Lightyear*, and their friends as they experience different challenges.

## Main Characters

- **Woody** – A cowboy doll and the leader of the toys.
- **Buzz Lightyear** – A space ranger action figure.
- **Jessie** – A cheerful cowgirl doll.
- **Rex** – A funny and nervous dinosaur.
- **Mr. Potato Head** – A toy with removable body parts.

## Why I Like It

I like this movie because it is funny, entertaining, and teaches the importance of friendship, loyalty, and teamwork.
